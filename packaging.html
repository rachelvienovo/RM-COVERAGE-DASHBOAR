Basis and caveats

  • Stock: RM_WH_Mgt › INV (on-hand at last sync; PACKAGING class, in pieces). Includes plant, -PD (production) and EXT/ALAE warehouses; excludes -IT and -QA locations.
  • Consumption: RM_WH_Mgt › Plant Consumption Breakdown, average monthly usage per plant. Horeb + Argao are combined as CEBU; ISABELA is shown as PFMC.
  • Pending: SBO Purchase Blanket Agreement › OPEN PBA + PO, balance quantity per warehouse. This is the unreceived balance of PBAs plus POs; no ETA is available, so pending is treated as arriving in full. PBAs past their end date are flagged and can be excluded.
  • Shortfall to target = target months × monthly consumption − stock − pending (already-ordered quantity is netted out). Targets and thresholds above are editable; the defaults are placeholders, not company policy.
  • Packaging: class PACKAGING; quantities are pieces. Warehouse BAC (Visayas) is counted under CEBU. Pending lines with a zero or negative balance (over-received) are ignored.
  • Regional days of cover treat each plant separately; stock is not transferred between plants in this model.
