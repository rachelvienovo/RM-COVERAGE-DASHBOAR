Basis and caveats

  • Stock: RM_WH_Mgt › INV (on-hand at last sync; /*STOCKNOTE*/). Includes plant, -PD (production) and EXT/ALAE warehouses; excludes -IT and -QA locations.
  • Cost: Inventory Audit copy (sheet 'Inventory Price'), the Avg Cost column per warehouse (weighted by that sheet's Ending Balance when a warehouse has several rows). Stock value = stock from RM_WH_Mgt x avg cost. The audit's Ending Balance is not used for stock, so it can differ slightly from RM_WH_Mgt; stock is shown as in RM_WH_Mgt. Warehouses with no audit cost use the item's average.
  • Consumption: RM_WH_Mgt › each plant's MIN_ sheet, AV W Fcasts consump (average weekly forecast consumption) × 4 = monthly. Plants map to warehouses as Bulacan → AC, Isabela → PFMIS, Bukidnon → BUKID, South → SOUTH, plus HOREB and ARGAO. CCPC is not included. Sub-warehouses roll up to their plant (AC-PD and ACEXT to AC, BUKID-PD and ALAE to BUKID, and so on).
  • Pending: SBO Purchase Blanket Agreement › OPEN PBA + PO, balance quantity per warehouse. This is the unreceived balance of PBAs plus POs; no ETA is available, so pending is treated as arriving in full. PBAs past their end date are flagged and can be excluded.
  • Shortfall to target = target months × monthly consumption − stock − pending (already-ordered quantity is netted out). Targets and thresholds above are editable; the defaults are placeholders, not company policy.
    • /*PKGNOTE*/
  • Days of cover treat each warehouse separately; stock is not transferred between plants in this model.
